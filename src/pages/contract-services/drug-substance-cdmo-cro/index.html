Drug substance buyer guide

Use these quick notes to evaluate API-focused partners without wading through a full outsourcing playbook.

What to ask a Drug Substance CDMO

Ask about containment/OEB limits, in-house analytics, scale-up track record, and which regulatory markets they actively serve.

  • What OEB/HPAPI ranges do you routinely handle, and how is containment validated?
  • Which analytics and impurity methods are in-house vs outsourced?
  • How do you manage scale-up from kilo lab to commercial campaigns?
  • Which markets have you supported recently (FDA, EMA, PMDA)?
Key terms
  • API: active pharmaceutical ingredient that becomes the drug substance.
  • Intermediate: upstream material used before the final API step.
  • HPAPI/OEB: potency classifications driving containment requirements.
  • GMP readiness: documentation, validation, and QA maturity needed for target markets.
Common pitfalls
  • Impurity profiles change at scale—confirm the partner has experience managing drift.
  • Process scalability gaps can delay GMP timelines; review pilot data early.
  • Tech transfer assumptions often hide missing analytics or raw material specs.

Drug substance FAQ

  • When should I involve a CRO in drug substance work?

    Bring in CRO support when you need specialized analytical development or early-stage research services that complement the CDMO scope.

  • Do I need a dedicated HPAPI facility?

    If potency or OEB limits require closed handling and validated containment, shortlist partners with dedicated suites and documented exposure controls.
