What is Drug Substance outsourcing (CDMO/CRO)?

Drug Substance refers to the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) or biologic material that delivers therapeutic effect. Drug Substance outsourcing covers API route development, scale-up, and GMP manufacturing of APIs and key intermediates.

A contract development manufacturing organization (CDMO) provides development plus manufacturing, while a CRO adds research and analytical support. Many biotech CDMO partners combine both, which is why CDMO/CRO appears together in this directory.

  • Choose a CDMO: when you need development + manufacturing under one quality system.
  • Choose a CRO: when you only need early-stage research or analytical services.
  • Choose a CDMO with CRO capabilities: when you need development, analytics, and manufacturing continuity.
  • Look for containment fit: match OEB/HPAPI ranges and validated exposure controls.

Drug Substance buyer guide

Use these quick notes to evaluate Drug Substance CDMO services without a full outsourcing playbook.

What to ask a Drug Substance CDMO

Confirm containment and in-house analytics before selecting a pharmaceutical CDMO company for Drug Substance development.

  • What OEB/HPAPI ranges do you routinely handle, and how is containment validated?
  • Which analytics and impurity methods are in-house vs outsourced?
  • How do you manage scale-up from kilo lab to commercial campaigns?
  • Which markets have you supported recently (FDA, EMA, PMDA)?
Key terms and partner types
  • Drug Substance: the active ingredient, including APIs and biologic materials.
  • CDMO: development + manufacturing services for Drug Substance programs.
  • CRO: research, analytical, or early-stage support that may sit alongside CDMO outsourcing.
  • HPAPI/OEB: potency classifications that drive containment requirements.
  • Tech transfer: the handoff of process and analytical methods into GMP manufacturing.
Common pitfalls
  • Impurity profiles change at scale—confirm the partner has experience managing drift.
  • Process scalability gaps can delay GMP timelines; review pilot data early.
  • Tech transfer assumptions often hide missing analytics or raw material specs.

Drug Substance FAQ

  • When should I involve a CRO in Drug Substance work?

    Use a CRO for early-stage research or specialized analytics, then move to CDMO services when you need development plus CDMO manufacturing.

  • Do I need a dedicated HPAPI facility?

    If potency or OEB limits require closed handling and validated containment, shortlist CDMO companies with dedicated suites.
