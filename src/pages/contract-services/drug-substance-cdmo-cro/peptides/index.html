What is peptide outsourcing (CDMO)?

Peptide outsourcing focuses on synthesizing peptide drug substance using solid-phase or solution-phase methods. A pharmaceutical CDMO develops efficient synthesis cycles, purifies the peptide to specification, and delivers GMP batches with verified identity and purity.

Typical steps include resin loading, coupling and deprotection cycles, purification, and lyophilization or drying. Key constraints often involve sequence length, aggregation, and tight impurity profiles.

When to choose a CDMO for peptides

  • You need specialized peptide synthesis and purification capability.
  • Modifications or conjugations require experienced process development.
  • Your program requires GMP-grade peptide supply for clinical or commercial use.

Common buyer requirements

  • Defined sequence, purity target, and acceptable impurity profile.
  • Scale expectations from grams to multi-kilogram campaigns.
  • Analytical confirmation of identity, potency, and stability.

Peptide CDMO services FAQ

Practical answers for buyers sourcing CDMO services for peptide drug substance manufacturing.

What does a peptide CDMO do for drug substance?

A peptide CDMO develops and manufactures peptide drug substance using solid-phase or solution-phase synthesis. Services include purification, analytical characterization, and GMP manufacturing.

What synthesis methods are common for peptide CDMO services?

Most peptide CDMOs use solid-phase peptide synthesis for short to medium sequences and solution-phase for longer or complex constructs. The method depends on sequence length, modifications, and scale.

What should I include in a peptide CDMO RFQ?

Include sequence, length, modifications, desired purity, batch size, and target markets. Add information about salt form, counterions, and analytical method preferences.

How do peptide CDMOs control purity and impurities?

Purity control relies on high-performance liquid chromatography, mass spectrometry, and stringent deprotection protocols. Ask about deletion sequences and oxidation control strategies.

Do peptide CDMOs handle modified or conjugated peptides?

Many do, including PEGylation, lipidation, or other conjugations. Confirm experience with your modification type and required analytics.

What batch sizes are typical for peptide CDMO manufacturing?

Peptide batches can range from grams for early-stage programs to multi-kilogram GMP campaigns. Provide forecasted demand to match reactor and purification capacity.

How do I evaluate GMP readiness for peptide drug substance?

Confirm GMP inspections, validated cleaning procedures, and impurity control documentation. Ensure the facility is experienced with peptide-specific analytical testing.

Are peptide CDMO services suitable for clinical trial supply?

Yes, many peptide CDMOs provide clinical and commercial supply. Verify timelines and stability study capability for your trial phase.

What are common pitfalls in peptide CDMO outsourcing?

Pitfalls include underestimating sequence complexity, inadequate impurity specifications, and unclear salt form requirements. Early alignment on analytics reduces rework.

Do peptide CDMOs support lyophilization?

Some CDMOs offer lyophilization or drying services for peptide drug substance. Confirm capacity and cycle development support if needed.

How important are analytical methods for peptide CDMO manufacturing?

Analytical methods are critical for confirming sequence, purity, and identity. In-house capabilities often reduce turnaround time for release testing.

Can a peptide CDMO help with process development and scale-up?

Yes, peptide CDMOs optimize synthesis cycles, resin loading, and purification to improve yield. Share lab-scale data to accelerate scale-up.

What regulatory documentation is typical for peptide drug substance?

CDMOs often support drug master file content, batch records, and analytical data packages. Confirm the exact documentation scope in your RFQ.

How do I shortlist peptide CDMO companies in this directory?

Use filters for region, stage, and services, then check experience with similar sequence length and modifications. Shortlist partners with strong purification and analytics.
