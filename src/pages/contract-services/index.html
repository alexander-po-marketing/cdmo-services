FAQ

What is the difference between a CMO and a CDMO?

A CDMO offers development plus manufacturing, while a CMO focuses on manufacturing.

How do I shortlist partners?

Use filters for dosage form, region, and certifications to build a targeted list.

Can I request multiple quotes at once?

Yes, you can send RFQs to multiple providers and track responses in one place.
